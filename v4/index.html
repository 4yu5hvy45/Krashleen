Pincode Launch PR Box

Pincode by Kunal Kapur
Brand storytellingPR strategyLaunch experience

The Idea

To amplify the launch of Pincode in Indore, we created a curated PR box inspired by the brand's identity of connection, culture, and discovery. Elements such as postcards, stamps, keepsakes, and personalized notes were thoughtfully integrated to transform the unboxing experience into a tangible extension of the brand

Objective

  • Build excitement around the launch
  • Create a memorable brand experience
  • Encourage social sharing and organic visibility
  • Strengthen brand recall through storytelling-led design

My Role

  • PR box ideation & concept development
  • Product sourcing & vendor coordination
  • Curated brand-aligned elements
  • Influencer & media distribution planning
  • Packaging & execution management

Impact

The PR box extended the launch experience beyond the event, creating a highly shareable brand touchpoint for creators, media, and guests. By incorporating postal-inspired elements that reflected Pincode's core identity, the activation strengthened brand recall, generated organic conversations, and reinforced the authenticity of the brand story.
